# Attorney.plus — Terms of Service

**Version v1, effective 2026-05-08 (prelaunch draft)**

> This is a prelaunch placeholder. Final terms must be reviewed by licensed counsel before any production launch.

## 1. Not a law firm

Attorney.plus is a technology platform. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Use of this platform does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## 2. The process

You agree that disputes started on this platform will follow this funnel:

1. **Quick decision (AI-mediated).** If both parties agree to the AI-mediated decision, it stands.
2. **Professional arbitration.** If either party disagrees, a professional arbitrator reviews the case. Total arbitration fee will not exceed $1,500.
3. **Attorney representation.** If arbitration fails, each party may engage a participating attorney. Each side pays a $1,500 platform fee to be matched.

## 3. Consent to platform process

By clicking "I agree to use Attorney.plus arbitration," you agree that the outcome of the AI-mediated quick decision is binding between the parties only when both parties have so agreed.

## 4. Privacy and audit

Your agreement events — timestamp, IP address, user-agent, and ToS version — are recorded immutably for audit purposes. You may request a copy of the audit record for your case at any time.

## 5. Prelaunch

No payments are processed during prelaunch. Account creation, case creation, and agreement events are real and stored. Payments and attorney matching are disabled.

## 6. Acceptable use

You agree not to misuse the platform, attempt to interfere with its operation, or submit fraudulent disputes.

## 7. Termination

We may suspend accounts that violate these terms. Audit records are retained even after account termination, anonymized as required by law.
